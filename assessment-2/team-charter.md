
# Agile Team Charter – QR Code Attendance System

## Introduction
The Agile Team Charter defines the shared purpose, responsibilities, communication methods, and working agreements of our QR Code Attendance System project team.

## Project Purpose
Our project focuses on developing a QR-code-based system to improve the process of recording and managing attendance.

## My Role and Responsibilities
My responsibility in the team was to clarify project requirements and assist in translating business needs into user stories.

## Team Collaboration
Our team used WhatsApp and email for communication and arranged regular meetings to discuss project progress, responsibilities, and challenges.
