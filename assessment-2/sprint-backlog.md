
# Sprint Backlog – QR Code Attendance System

## Introduction
The Sprint Backlog identifies the work selected for a particular sprint in the QR Code Attendance System project. It helps the development team organise tasks, monitor progress, and work towards a shared sprint goal.

## Sprint Planning
During sprint planning, the development team identifies the backlog items that can be completed during the sprint. Tasks are organised according to their importance and the team's available capacity.
