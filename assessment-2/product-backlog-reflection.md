
# Reflection on Product Backlog

## Situation
During the development of our QR Code Attendance System, our team needed a structured way to organise the system requirements and determine which features should receive attention first.

## Task
My responsibility involved helping clarify system requirements and translating business needs into user stories. I also needed to understand how those requirements could be organised and prioritised within the Product Backlog.

## Action
I reviewed the identified system requirements and considered how each feature would contribute to the overall attendance process. I focused on understanding the importance of core functionality, such as QR code generation, scanning, and attendance recording. I also considered how backlog priorities could guide the team's development activities.

## Result
This activity improved my understanding of Agile planning and requirements prioritisation. I learned that a Product Backlog provides a clear overview of the work needed and helps teams focus on delivering the most valuable features first.

## Personal Learning
I learned that effective backlog management requires clear requirements, communication, and regular review. In future projects, I would aim to improve the clarity of backlog items and ensure that priorities are updated as project requirements change.
