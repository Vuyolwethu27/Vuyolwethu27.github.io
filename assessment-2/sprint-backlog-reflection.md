# Reflection on Sprint Backlog

## Situation
During the QR Code Attendance System project, our team needed to organise development tasks into manageable pieces of work. Sprint Backlog planning provided a way to understand the work required to achieve specific development goals.

## Task
My responsibility involved clarifying project requirements and helping translate business needs into user stories. I also needed to understand how these user stories could be broken down into development tasks and planned according to project priorities.

## Action
I reviewed the system requirements and considered the tasks needed to implement features such as QR code generation, scanning, and attendance recording. I examined how development tasks could be organised, prioritised, and monitored to support progress towards the project's objectives.

## Result
This experience improved my understanding of Agile sprint planning and task management. I learned that breaking requirements into smaller tasks helps teams estimate work, identify dependencies, and monitor development progress.

## Personal Learning
I learned that effective sprint planning requires realistic goals, clear responsibilities, and regular communication. In future projects, I would encourage clearer task estimates and frequent progress reviews to help identify delays earlier.
