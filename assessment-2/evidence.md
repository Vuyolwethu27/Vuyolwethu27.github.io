
# Reflection on Agile Team Charter

## Situation
During the development of our QR Code Attendance System, our group needed to establish a clear understanding of each member's responsibilities, expectations, and communication methods. The Agile Team Charter helped us organise how we would work together throughout the project.

## Task
My responsibility was to help clarify project requirements and translate business needs into user stories. I also needed to understand my role within the team and contribute to our shared project objectives. I also had to contribute to the documentation of the project's progress, the designing of the project and also the development of it.

## Action
I reviewed the agreed team responsibilities and participated in project discussions to better understand the requirements of the QR Code Attendance System. I focused on identifying user needs and supporting the development of clear user stories. I also worked within the communication and collaboration arrangements established by the team. I also contributed to designing and developing the system.

## Result
This experience helped me understand the importance of clearly defined responsibilities and communication in Agile projects. I learned that a team charter provides a foundation for accountability, teamwork, and effective project coordination. It also helped me appreciate how my requirements-related responsibilities contribute to the success of the overall project.

## Personal Development
The Team Charter activity strengthened my understanding of teamwork, responsibility, and Agile collaboration. In future projects, I would encourage regular reviews of team agreements to ensure that responsibilities and communication methods remain effective.
