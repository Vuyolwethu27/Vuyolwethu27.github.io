
# Reflection on Agile Logbook

## Situation
During the development of the QR Code Attendance System, our team needed to keep track of project activities, progress, challenges, and decisions. The Agile Logbook provided a way to document development activities and review the work completed over time.

## Task
My responsibility was to contribute to the project through requirements clarification and user story development. I also needed to understand how project progress could be documented and monitored using Agile practices.

## Action
I reviewed the project activities and considered how meeting discussions, task updates, and development progress could be documented in the Agile Logbook. I focused on recording or reviewing information relevant to requirements, user stories, and my individual responsibilities.

## Result
This activity helped me understand the importance of keeping accurate project records. I learned that an Agile Logbook can improve accountability, communication, and progress monitoring by providing a record of decisions, challenges, and completed activities.

## Personal Learning
I learned that documenting progress consistently helps teams identify problems early and reflect on their development process. In future projects, I would aim to maintain more detailed and regular records of my contributions, challenges, and lessons learned.
